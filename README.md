## Hi, I'm Tamás 👋

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Szeged with a strong interest in full-stack web development, backend systems, and software engineering.

What I enjoy most about programming is turning an idea into a complete application. From planning the project and designing the database to writing documentation and implementing the final product, I enjoy being involved in every stage of development.

I like working on projects that challenge and encourage me to learn something new. Rather than avoiding difficult problems, I enjoy breaking them down into smaller pieces and finding practical solutions.

I'm always open to learning new technologies, improving my existing skills, and taking on projects that push me beyond my current knowledge.
